## Kickoff agenda (YYYY-MM-DD)

1. Goal and why (from charter)
2. Project structure and how each project affects the others (program)
3. Each person's role (RACI walk-through)
4. Communication plan: what goes out, when, to whom
5. First-pass risks — everyone contributes
6. Who is missing from the stakeholder list?
7. Open questions and owners

## Leadership sync format (program)

Round-robin, one project at a time: status colour, blockers, anything
affecting another project. New major issues parked until the round is done
so unaffected people can leave.
